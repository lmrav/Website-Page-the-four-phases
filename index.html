Around the Utero

  • Menstrual
  • Follicular
  • Ovulation
  • Luteal

Menstrual

This is the first stage of the cycle where the uterus sheds its lining, causing bleeding through the vagina.

Follicular

During this phase, the ovaries stimulate an egg to fully develop. The uterine lining also thickens.

Ovulation

During this phase, a mature follicle in the ovary ruptures, releasing an egg into the fallopian tube. This process is triggered by a surge in luteinizing hormone (LH) and estrogen.

Luteal

Prepares your uterine lining for pregnancy by making it thicker. The thick lining is the ideal environment for a fertilized egg to implant and a pregnancy to grow, and it ends when you get your period.
